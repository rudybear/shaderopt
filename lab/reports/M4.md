## Takeaways for this device class
1. The only wins above the gates came from **algorithmic rewrites** (fewer texture fetches). ALU-side transforms (exact rewrites, hoisting, polynomials, f16) are within noise on the RTX PRO 6000; the driver and the hardware already make them free.
2. Memory-traffic transforms (formats, resolution) move chain time by 1..8% and are the app-level proposals worth carrying to mobile, where tilers pay much more for bandwidth.
3. The pipeline is now complete end to end for the next device class: every transform is a typed edit-op with a CPU prediction and a measured verdict.

Proposals for the app (none applied): quarter-resolution bloom if content is smooth; R11G11B10F bloom intermediates; bilinear-assisted 5-tap blur; 2x2 PCF.
