# M2 report: classification and exact canonicalization (desktop)

Date: 2026-09-25. Device: RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell, driver 580.178.04. Per-shader detail: `lab/reports/<shader>.classification.md` and `<shader>.canonical.md`; machine-readable: `lab/analysis/<shader>.json`, `<shader>.canon.json`.

## 1. Classification

`shader-ir analyze` (rate dataflow with control dependence, backward sink marking, sampler coordinate provenance, source lines from `-g` builds) plus the CPU model for ranges (stride 4 over two train scenarios) and per-site f16 sensitivity (FLIP vs the unperturbed f32 model, one train scenario, storage-format quantized).

| shader | float sites | pixel | uniform | sink sites (address/control/discard) | lossy candidates | f16 on ALL candidates: FLIP mean / p99 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| tonemap_aces | 49 | 35 | 9 | 1 (1/0/0) | 48 | 0.0000 / 0.0000 |
| fxaa | 215 | 190 | 43 | 180 (138/79/0) | 40 | 0.0000 / 0.0000 |
| gaussian_blur_h/v | 33 | 13 | 15 | 12 (10/4/0) | 26 | 0.0004 / 0.0024, 0.0006 / 0.0031 |
| bloom_threshold | 40 | 28 | 13 | 1 | 39 | 0.0008 / 0.0052 |
| bloom_composite | 25 | 22 | 4 | 2 | 23 | 0.0009 / 0.0145 |
| deferred_lit | 74 | 64 | 18 | 44 (20/31/8) | 48 | 0.0006 / 0.0094 |
| color_grade | 60 | 32 | 25 | 1 | 59 | 0.0075 / 0.0244 |
| vignette_grain | 59 | 46 | 11 | 1 | 58 | 0.1037 / 0.1500 |

Findings:
- **Hard sinks dominate FXAA.** 180 of 215 float sites feed a texture coordinate or a branch; only 40 sites (lines 30-34: `lumaL`, `rangeL`, `blendL`, the final `mix`) are lossy candidates. The two branch conditions (line 25 early-out, line 43 select) are the cost story: FXAA runs 23.8 us on edges and 56.2 us on noise.
- **f16 UVs are catastrophic, as the brief predicted.** Rounding every float site including the uv chain in tonemap gives FLIP p99 0.57; excluding sinks, the same shader shows 0.0000. This was the first run's mistake and is now the rule in the tool.
- **Two shaders are f16-free.** tonemap_aces and fxaa show no output change at all with every candidate rounded to f16 (8-bit sRGB outputs absorb the rounding). Blur, threshold, composite and deferred_lit stay within the default budget (p99 <= 0.05); color_grade is within budget but close on mean (0.0075 vs 0.01).
- **Precision-fragile:** vignette_grain (hash amplification), FLIP mean 0.10 with everything demoted. Site-level results show which sites are safe.
- **Uniform-rate work:** blur weights `exp(-0.5*x*x/(sigma*sigma))` and `wsum` are uniform-rate (M4 hoisting targets, 15 sites); color_grade has 25 uniform sites (the matrix and lift/gamma/gain preprocessing).
- **Ranges:** no NaN/Inf or out-of-f16-range intermediate observed on the train scenarios for any shader (the NaN/Inf holdout scenarios are not used for ranges by design).
- **Pass graph:** no fusable pair in the current chains. bloom composite reads the half-resolution bloom at uv (resolution differs); blur_v reads blur_h with offsets; threshold reads the scene input. Fusion candidates need same-resolution, own-pixel consumers, which the corpus does not yet contain.

## 2. Exact canonicalization

Pipelines: `canon_exact` = fold, dce, cse, ident, unroll, select; `canon_full` adds divconst and powspec (`ulp` class). Every variant validated with spirv-val, then verified bit-identical to the original in the CPU f64 and f32 models on a train scenario, then measured interleaved with the baseline (2 rounds x 20 samples x 8 executions).

| shader | ops (exact pipeline) | instructions | CPU verification | GPU effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| gaussian_blur_h/v | 78 (unroll 9x, 58 cse, 18 fold) | 63 -> 269 | bit-identical | none (+0.16% / -0.12%, CI includes 0) |
| deferred_lit | 98 (unroll 3x3 PCF, 51 cse, 40 fold, 2 select) | 166 -> 306 | bit-identical | none (-0.08%) |
| fxaa | 50 cse | 329 -> 279 | bit-identical | none (+0.03%) |
| color_grade | 7 | 84 -> 77 | bit-identical | none |
| tonemap_aces | 6 fold | 66 -> 60 | bit-identical | none |
| vignette_grain, bloom_threshold, bloom_composite | 7, 4, 2 | | bit-identical | none |

**Result: the NVIDIA driver already performs every exact rewrite in this set.** No variant moved the median by more than 0.2% and every CI includes zero. This is logged so the M5 search never proposes these on this device class; on mobile compilers the same variants must be re-measured (Mali and Adreno compilers are known to unroll and CSE less aggressively). Not tried yet: store-to-load forwarding (needed before post-unroll constant folding of the blur weights), `powspec` (no constant exponents in the corpus), `divconst` beyond one site.
